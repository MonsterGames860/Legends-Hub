The gizmo appears in the viewport when a cube is selected with the 'Select/Move' tool. In Move/Rotate mode, drag the colored axis arrows (red=X, green=Y, blue=Z) — only that axis changes. In Scale (R) mode, axis-colored squares on 6 faces (red=X, green=Y, blue=Z) and yellow cube handles on 8 corners appear: dragging a face handle outward enlarges only that face (inward shrinks it); dragging a corner handle uniformly scales all three axes — the opposite face/corner always stays fixed. Handles stay locked to the cube's own orientation even as the camera rotates.
